Kept

The yearly cost of the subscriptions and bills you keep paying.

Kept is a list that lives on your phone. You type each charge once. The app turns it into a monthly total. Pro adds the full year, a reminder three days before the renewal, and a spreadsheet when you want one.

What you get

  1. Add a charge Name, amount, how often it bills, and the next date.
  2. See the month Three charges are free, with the monthly total on the first screen.
  3. Keep the year Pro removes the cap, shows the yearly number, reminds you, and exports a CSV.

Pro

Best value

Yearly

One payment for the year. This is the plan the app highlights.

Monthly

Renews each month until you cancel it in Google Play.

Lifetime

Pay once. If a subscription is still active, cancel it in Google Play so you are not billed twice.

The app is free to install. Prices for Pro are shown by Google Play and can differ by country. There are no ads inside Kept.

Privacy

Effective October 20, 2026. Kept is published by One Colea for Android.

The charges you enter stay on that phone: the name, amount, currency, how often it bills, and the next charge date. Kept also stores, on the device, whether Pro is unlocked and the identifiers it needs to schedule reminders.

A purchase or a restore goes through Google Play. Google processes the payment. Kept does not receive your card number, and One Colea does not run an account system or a server for your list.

If you allow notifications, the phone shows a reminder about three days before a renewal. If you export, Kept writes a CSV and hands it to the share sheet. It goes only to the app you choose.

Kept does not include advertising or analytics. It is not directed at children. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone, except for a copy your own Android backup may still hold.

Questions about this policy go to the developer email address on the Kept listing in Google Play.
